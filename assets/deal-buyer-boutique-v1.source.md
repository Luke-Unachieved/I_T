## Selected composition

A left-side glazed shopfront, coastal light, a sage-green recessed display wall and an empty pale-oak island. The tabletop meets the back wall at about 54–56% of image height and reaches the front edge around 95%; the central working surface stays available for the current order's live samples. No customers, transaction details or merchandise are baked into the background. This source record is not a whole-game or target-device visual acceptance.

## Generation prompt (first pass, no image input)

Create ONE original professional game environment art background, landscape 16:9. Absolutely no reference images. Setting: a small contemporary independent design buyer's concept shop in a coastal city, seen at the customer's eye level from straight across a merchandising island. Quiet modern realism, painterly architectural visualization, tactile pale oak, sand-toned plaster, linen, a restrained ink-green joinery accent; coherent cool daylight and a patch of warmer late afternoon light coming from a left side window with fine leaf shadows. An original composition: tall glazed street door and glimpses of abstract streetscape toward the far left; a shallow sage-green recessed niche off-center on the right with two empty oak floating shelves, one modest stack of closed neutral boxes at the periphery and a small matte carafe; the center has calm warm wall and depth, not a symmetrical showroom. A single beautifully made pale honey oak counter nearly spans the scene, its BACK edge around 60 percent of image height and its front edge near 90 percent; the middle two thirds of the counter must be genuinely EMPTY and visually quiet, with enough visible surface for live game-rendered samples and animated products. Naturally uneven subtle wood grain, no loud patterns, grounded lighting and realistic construction, no conspicuous fantasy, no template icons. Aim for a believable, inviting working shop, not a sterile luxury hotel or staged catalogue scene. No people, hands, buyers, silhouettes, lamps, cups, textile samples or sample trays on the counter, no merchandise in the center, no graphics, UI, lettering, signs, logos or watermarks. No depth-of-field blur. Entire frame is a finished game-ready background, no page or margins.

## Refinement prompt (only the first generated image as input)

Refine the just-generated original concept-shop environment into its production game background, preserving its naturally lit sage niche, left-side coastal street glazing, small peripheral olive tree, neutral merchandise-free area, restrained materials and modern realism. CRUCIAL COMPOSITION FIX: the rendered products in the game need a VERY DEEP uninterrupted empty tabletop, not a narrow sliver above a giant counter front. Foreground FRONT edge should be low at around 90–95% of the image height or just beyond frame; cabinet FRONT below tabletop occupies at MOST 6% of height. Keep the far wall/window verticals architectural. Actual objects will be added in code separately, so literally no hands, people, goods, tabletop clutter, lamps, cloth, cups, other merchandise or text are allowed on this vast foreground surface. Rework camera and back wall scale as needed. No logos, words or watermarks, same wide 16:9 single composition, high quality game background.
